Reading

1. Roots and coefficients: Vieta's formulas

For the quadratic ax² + bx + c with roots r and s: r + s = −b/a and rs = c/a. For a cubic x³ + px² + qx + r₀ with roots α, β, γ: the sum of roots is −p, the sum of pairwise products is q, the product is −r₀. For x³ − 6x² + 11x − 6 the roots are 1, 2, 3, with sum 6, pairwise sum 2 + 3 + 6 = 11 and product 6 (a computer solve confirms).

Using Vieta. You can find symmetric expressions without finding the roots. For x² − 5x + 3 with roots a, b: a² + b² = (a + b)² − 2ab = 25 − 6 = 19, and 1/a + 1/b = (a + b)/ab = 5/3. For the cubic above, a² + b² + c² = 6² − 2 · 11 = 14.

Building a polynomial from its roots. (x − 1)²(x + 2) has roots 1, 1, −2 and expands to x³ − 3x + 2.

Try it

The roots of x² − 7x + 2 are p and q. Find p² + q² and p³ + q³.

Answer. p + q = 7, pq = 2. p² + q² = 49 − 4 = 45; p³ + q³ = 343 − 3 · 2 · 7 = 301.

2. Newton's sums

Let pₖ = rᵏ + sᵏ + tᵏ be the power sums of the roots and e₁, e₂, e₃ the elementary symmetric sums (here 6, 11, 6). Newton's identities give p₁ = e₁ = 6, p₂ = e₁p₁ − 2e₂ = 36 − 22 = 14, p₃ = e₁p₂ − e₂p₁ + 3e₃ = 84 − 66 + 18 = 36 (indeed 1 + 8 + 27 = 36), and for k > 3: pₖ = e₁pₖ₋₁ − e₂pₖ₋₂ + e₃pₖ₋₃. So p₄ = 6 · 36 − 11 · 14 + 6 · 6 = 98 (1 + 16 + 81 = 98).

Why it is useful. It computes the sum of high powers of roots of an ugly polynomial using only its coefficients, without solving it.

Try it

If x² − x − 1 = 0 has roots φ and ψ, find φ⁴ + ψ⁴.

Answer. p₁ = 1, e₂ = −1: p₂ = 1 + 2 = 3, p₃ = p₂ + p₁ = 4, p₄ = p₃ + p₂ = 7.

3. Remainder and factor theorems; division

Dividing P(x) by (x − a) leaves remainder P(a). So x − a is a factor exactly when P(a) = 0. The remainder when x³ + 2x + 5 is divided by x − 2 is 8 + 4 + 5 = 17. Long division of x³ − 2x² + 4x − 8 by x − 2 gives x² + 4 with remainder 0; dividing 2x³ + 3x² − x + 5 by x + 1 gives quotient 2x² + x − 2 and remainder 7 = P(−1).

Evaluation at 1 and −1. The sum of the coefficients of P is P(1), and the sum of the coefficients of even powers is (P(1) + P(−1))/2. For P = (2x − 1)⁵: P(1) = 1, P(−1) = −243, so the even-power coefficient sum is −121.

Rational roots. For 6x³ − 11x² + 6x − 1 the possible rational roots are ±1, ±1/2, ±1/3, ±1/6; testing finds 1, 1/2 and 1/3 are all roots.

Try it

Find the remainder when x¹⁰ − 3x + 1 is divided by x + 1.

Answer. P(−1) = 1 + 3 + 1 = 5.

4. A longer problem

Problem. The cubic x³ − 3x² + 5x − 7 has roots a, b, c. Find a² + b² + c², a³ + b³ + c³ and 1/a + 1/b + 1/c.

Solution. e₁ = 3, e₂ = 5, e₃ = 7. p₂ = 9 − 10 = −1. p₃ = e₁p₂ − e₂p₁ + 3e₃ = −3 − 15 + 21 = 3. 1/a + 1/b + 1/c = e₂/e₃ = 5/7. (p₂ = −1 is negative because two roots are complex.)

Reflection. The negative p₂ is a good check of the idea: the roots are not all real, so there is no contradiction.

5. Mistakes, checks and how to practise

Common mistakes. (1) Sign errors in Vieta (alternating signs). (2) Forgetting to divide by the leading coefficient. (3) Using Newton's sums with the wrong sign convention. (4) Missing repeated roots.

Checks. Test with a polynomial whose roots you know, like (x − 1)(x − 2)(x − 3).

How to practise. Write e₁, e₂, e₃ explicitly before computing. In Unlimited practice, rotate the Vieta and Newton families.

More worked examples: Worked examples

For x³ − 6x² + 11x − 6 = 0 the roots are 1, 2, 3, with e₁ = 6, e₂ = 11, e₃ = 6. Newton's sums: p₁ = 6, p₂ = e₁p₁ − 2e₂ = 36 − 22 = 14 and p₃ = e₁p₂ − e₂p₁ + 3e₃ = 84 − 66 + 18 = 36, which match 1 + 4 + 9 = 14 and 1 + 8 + 27 = 36. The Remainder Theorem: the remainder of P(x) = x³ − 2x + 5 on division by x − 2 is P(2) = 9. By the Factor Theorem x − 3 divides x³ − 27 because 3³ − 27 = 0.

Try it

The roots of x² − 4x + 1 are r and s. Find r² + s².

Answer. 16 − 2 = 14.

Try it

Find the remainder when x⁴ + 1 is divided by x + 1.

Answer. (−1)⁴ + 1 = 2.

More worked examples: A second longer problem

Problem. The cubic x³ − 7x + 6 has integer roots. Find them and verify with Vieta. Solution. Trying divisors of 6 gives x = 1: 1 − 7 + 6 = 0. Dividing, x³ − 7x + 6 = (x − 1)(x² + x − 6) = (x − 1)(x − 2)(x + 3), with roots 1, 2, −3. Vieta: sum = 0 (the x² coefficient is 0) ✓, product = −6 (= −constant term) ✓.

Reflection. Always try the divisors of the constant term first.

Problem x18. The roots of x² − 10x + 21 = 0 are r and s. Find r² + s².

HintUse r + s and rs.
Answer58
