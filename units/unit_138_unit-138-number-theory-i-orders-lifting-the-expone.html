1. Orders and Euler's theorem

If gcd(a, m) = 1 the order of a mod m is the least k with aᵏ ≡ 1 and divides φ(m). The order of 3 mod 7 is 6 (3 is a primitive root), of 2 mod 7 is 3. To compute 7^2026 mod 10: the order of 7 mod 10 is 4 and 2026 ≡ 2 mod 4, so the answer is 7² = 49 → 9.

Try it

Order of 2 mod 7?

Answer. 3.

Try it

Last digit of 3^2027?

Answer. 3^2027: 2027 ≡ 3 mod 4, so 27 → 7.

2. Lifting the exponent and quadratic residues

LTE. If p is an odd prime dividing a − 1, then v_p(aⁿ − 1) = v_p(a − 1) + v_p(n). Example: v₃(10²⁷ − 1) = 2 + 3 = 5. For p = 2 and n even: v₂(aⁿ − 1) = v₂(a − 1) + v₂(a + 1) + v₂(n) − 1, e.g. v₂(3¹⁶ − 1) = 1 + 2 + 4 − 1 = 6. Squares. Mod an odd prime p there are (p − 1)/2 non-zero squares, and −1 is a square iff p ≡ 1 mod 4.

Try it

v₅(6²⁵ − 1)?

Answer. 1 + 2 = 3.

Try it

Is −1 a square mod 13?

Answer. Yes: 5² = 25 ≡ −1.

3. A worked problem

Problem. Find the smallest positive x with x ≡ 2 (mod 3) and x ≡ 3 (mod 5). Solution. Numbers ≡ 3 mod 5 are 3, 8, 13; 8 ≡ 2 mod 3, so x = 8.

Check. 8 = 3 · 2 + 2 ✓ and 8 = 5 + 3 ✓.

Problem p1. Find the order of 2 modulo 31 (the least k ≥ 1 with 2^k ≡ 1).

HintThe order divides φ(31) = 30.
Answer5
Method: Divisors of φ

Test the divisors of 30: the first power of 2 that is 1 mod 31 is 5.

Method: Powers

List 2, 2², … mod 31 until 1 appears: at exponent 5.

Problem p12. How many residues r in {0, 1, …, 10} are squares modulo 11 (including 0)?

Hintx and −x give the same square.
Answer6
Method: Pairing

The non-zero squares come from the pairs ±x: (11 − 1)/2 = 5 of them, plus 0: 6.

Method: List

Compute x² mod 11 for x = 0, …, 10 and count distinct values: 6.

Source: Technique from Khurmi ch. 5–6, 9.2 (original problem)

Problem p16. Find the smallest positive integer x with x ≡ 2 (mod 3) and x ≡ 3 (mod 5).

HintList numbers ≡ 3 (mod 5) and test them mod 3.
Answer8
Method: Chinese Remainder

The solution is unique mod 15; combining the congruences gives x = 8.

Method: Search

The candidates 3, 8, 13 ≡ 3 mod 5; the first with x ≡ 2 mod 3 is 8.

Problem p20. For how many primes p < 30 does x² + 1 ≡ 0 (mod p) have a solution?

HintOnly p = 2 and primes ≡ 1 (mod 4) work.
Answer5
Method: Quadratic residues

−1 is a square mod p exactly when p = 2 or p ≡ 1 (mod 4); counting such primes below 30 gives 5.

Method: Direct test

Test x = 0, …, p − 1 for every prime below 30: 5 primes have a solution.

Source: Technique from Khurmi ch. 5–6, 9.2 (original problem)

Problem p23. How many integers from 1 to 60 are coprime to 6?

HintUse inclusion–exclusion over the prime factors of 6.
Answer20
Method: Inclusion–exclusion

Counting 60 minus multiples of each prime factor plus multiples of products gives 20.

Method: Direct

Check gcd(t, 6) for each t ≤ 60: 20 integers qualify.
