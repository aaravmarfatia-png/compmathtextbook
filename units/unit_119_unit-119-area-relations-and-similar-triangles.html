Reading

1. Same height, same base

Two triangles with the same height have areas in the ratio of their bases; two with the same base have areas in the ratio of their heights. Triangles between the same two parallel lines with the same base have equal area.

Worked example. In triangle ABC, D lies on BC with BD : DC = 2 : 3. Triangles ABD and ADC share the height from A, so their areas are in the ratio 2 : 3. If the area of ABC is 30, then ABD has area 12 and ADC has area 18.

Medians. A median splits a triangle into two equal-area triangles because the bases are equal.

2. Similar triangles and scale factors

If two figures are similar with ratio k of lengths, their areas have ratio k² and volumes k³. A line parallel to a side of a triangle cuts off a similar triangle.

Worked example 1. D and E are the midpoints of AB and AC. Triangle ADE is similar to ABC with ratio ½, so its area is ¼ of the whole, and the trapezoid DBCE has ¾.

Worked example 2. A line parallel to the base cuts a triangle of area 100 into a small triangle and a trapezoid, with the small triangle having area 36. The ratio of areas is 36/100, so the ratio of lengths is 6/10 = 3/5.

3. Trapezoid diagonals

In a trapezoid with parallel sides a and b, the diagonals meet and form four triangles. The two triangles on the parallel sides are similar with ratio a : b, so their areas are in the ratio a² : b², and the two side triangles each have area proportional to ab. In units of the smallest: a², ab, ab, b².

Worked example. If a = 2 and b = 3, the four areas are in proportion 4 : 6 : 6 : 9, total 25 = (a + b)². If the triangle on the shorter base has area 8, the whole trapezoid has area 8 × 25/4 = 50.

Cevians. Using areas, you can find the ratio in which cevians divide each other: add the areas of subtriangles ('area ratio' method) and use the same-height rule.

4. Mistakes, checks and how to practise

Common mistakes. Using the length ratio where the area ratio is needed. Choosing a base and height that do not correspond. Forgetting that the ratio of areas of similar triangles is the square.

Checks. Make sure the pieces add up to the whole.

Practice. Label every area with a letter and write down the same-height ratios.

Worked examples with Try-its: Area ratios

If DE is parallel to BC in triangle ABC with AD/AB = 2/5, then triangle ADE is similar to ABC and has (2/5)² = 4/25 of its area. Triangles with the same height have areas in the ratio of their bases, and a median splits a triangle into two equal areas.

Try it

Triangles ABC and ADE are similar with ratio 3 : 7. If [ABC] = 98, find [ADE].

Answer. 98 · 9/49 = 18.

Worked examples with Try-its: The trapezoid diagonals

In a trapezoid with parallel sides 2 and 6 and height 4 (area 16), the diagonals meet and make four triangles. The triangles on the parallel sides have areas 1 and 9, which are in the ratio 2² : 6², and the two side triangles each have area 3 (the geometric mean of 1 and 9). Check: 1 + 9 + 3 + 3 = 16.

Try it

In a trapezoid whose parallel sides are 3 and 5, what is the ratio of the areas of the triangles on those sides formed by the diagonals?

Answer. 9 : 25.

Worked examples with Try-its: A longer problem

Problem. A line parallel to the base of a triangle divides it into a triangle and a trapezoid of equal area. At what fraction of the height from the apex is the line? Solution. The small triangle has half the area, so the similarity ratio is 1/√2 ≈ 0.707.

Reflection. Area scales with the square of the ratio, so take square roots to recover lengths.

More worked examples: Worked examples

If DE ∥ BC with AD/AB = 3/8 then [ADE]/[ABC] = 9/64 and the trapezoid DBCE has area 55/64 of the whole. Two triangles with equal bases and heights in ratio 2 : 3 have areas in the same ratio. A median divides a triangle into two equal areas, and the three medians cut it into six equal areas.

Try it

A line parallel to the base of a triangle cuts off a triangle with 1/4 of the area. What fraction of the height is that?

Answer. 1/2.

Try it

Two similar triangles have perimeters 12 and 18. What is the ratio of their areas?

Answer. 4 : 9.

More worked examples: A second longer problem

Problem. In triangle ABC, D, E, F are the midpoints of the sides. Show [DEF] = [ABC]/4. Solution. Triangle DEF is similar to ABC with ratio 1/2 (midline theorem), so its area is (1/2)² = 1/4 of [ABC].

Check. The four small triangles are congruent, so each has area [ABC]/4.

Problem p1. In triangle ABC, D is the midpoint of BC and E is the midpoint of AD. The area of ABC is 48. Find the area of triangle BED.

HintHalve twice.
Answer12
Method: Medians

The median AD splits ABC into two triangles of area 24; E is the midpoint of AD, so BED has half the area of ABD: 12.

Method: Coordinates

With B = (0, 0), C = (8, 0), A = (0, 12): D = (4, 0), E = (2, 6), area of BED = (1/2)(4)(6) = 12.

Source: Zeitz, The Art and Craft of Problem Solving, ch. 8 (Geometry)

Problem p2. The three medians of a triangle of area 60 divide it into six small triangles. What is the area of each?

HintThey are equal.
Answer10
Method: Equal areas

60/6 = 10.

Method: Reason

Each small triangle has a base that is a half-side and the same height as its neighbour, so all six are equal.

Source: Zeitz, The Art and Craft of Problem Solving, ch. 8 (Geometry)

Problem p3. P is a point inside parallelogram ABCD of area 40. Find [PAB] + [PCD].

HintThe heights from P to AB and to CD add up to the height of the parallelogram.
Answer20
Method: Heights

[PAB] + [PCD] = (1/2)·AB·(h₁ + h₂) = (1/2)·AB·h = 20.

Method: Special case

If P is a vertex such as A, then [PAB] = 0 and [PCD] = [ACD] = 20.

Source: Zeitz, The Art and Craft of Problem Solving, ch. 8 (Geometry)

Problem p9. In triangle ABC, AB = 6, AC = 9 and the angle bisector from A meets BC at D. The area of ABC is 50. Find the area of triangle ABD.

HintBD:DC = AB:AC = 2:3.
Answer20
Method: Ratio

The triangles ABD and ADC have the same height from A, so their areas are in ratio BD:DC = 2:3. Therefore [ABD] = 2/5 · 50 = 20.

Method: Equal distances

The distance from D to AB equals the distance from D to AC, so the areas are in ratio AB:AC = 2:3, the same result.

Problem p12. Parallelogram ABCD has area 48 and M is the midpoint of BC. Find the area of triangle ABM.

HintCompare with triangle ABC.
Answer12
Method: Halves

Triangle ABC is half the parallelogram, 24, and ABM is half of ABC: 12.

Method: Base-height

Triangle ABM has base AB and a height half that of the parallelogram: ½ · AB · (h/2) = 1/4 of AB · h = 12.

HintAreas scale as the square of the ratio.
Answer64
Method: Area ratio

The areas are in the ratio 25 : 64, so the larger area is 25 · 64/25 = 64.

The linear factor is 8/5, so the area factor is 64/25; 25 · 64/25 = 64.

Problem x19. Find the area of the triangle with sides 10, 17 and 21.

HintHeron's formula.
Answer84
Method: Heron

s = 24, so K = √(24 · 14 · 7 · 3) = 84.

Method: Altitude

The altitude to the side 21 is 2K/21 = 8, so the area is ½ · 21 · 8 = 84.
