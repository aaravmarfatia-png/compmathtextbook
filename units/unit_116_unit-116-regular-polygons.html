Reading

1. Angles, sides and area

A regular n-gon has interior angle (n − 2) × 180°/n, exterior angle 360°/n, and n(n − 3)/2 diagonals. With circumradius R: side s = 2R sin(π/n), apothem a = R cos(π/n), area = ½ n R² sin(2π/n) = ½ × perimeter × apothem.

Worked example 1 (hexagon, side 1). R = 1, area = 6 × (√3/4) = 3√3/2 ≈ 2.598. Example 2 (octagon, side 1). Area = 2(1 + √2) ≈ 4.828. Example 3. A regular octagon has 8 × 5/2 = 20 diagonals; interior angle 135°.

2. Diagonals and the golden ratio

In a regular pentagon with side 1 the diagonal has length φ = (1 + √5)/2 ≈ 1.618. The diagonals divide each other in the golden ratio. The product of the distances from one vertex of a regular n-gon inscribed in a unit circle to all the other vertices equals n. For the pentagon: (2 sin 36° × 2 sin 72°)² = (1.1756 × 1.9021)² = 2.2361² = 5 ✓.

Roots of unity. The vertices of a regular n-gon in the complex plane are the nth roots of unity. The identity Π_{k=1}^{n−1}(1 − ω^k) = n explains the product above.

Worked example. A regular hexagon with side 2 has long diagonals 4 and short diagonals 2√3.

3. Constructions and tiling

Regular polygons tile the plane alone only for n = 3, 4, 6 (interior angles 60°, 90°, 120° divide 360°). Combinations of several kinds of polygon around each vertex also work, provided the angles add to 360°: for example 3.6.3.6 (60° + 120° + 60° + 120° = 360°) and 4.8.8 (90° + 135° + 135° = 360°).

Constructible polygons. A regular n-gon is constructible with compass and straightedge for n = 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 16, 17, …; not 7, 9, 11, 13, 14.

Worked example. The central angle for a regular 12-gon is 30°, so each side subtends 30° at the centre, and its area is 3R².

4. Mistakes, checks and how to practise

Common mistakes. Using degrees where radians are required in the area formula. Confusing the apothem with the circumradius. Counting diagonals as n(n − 1)/2 instead of n(n − 3)/2.

Checks. Test your area formula on a square (n = 4, R = 1: area 2).

Practice. Split the polygon into n isosceles triangles from the centre.

Worked examples with Try-its: Angles, area and radii

A regular n-gon has interior angle (n − 2) · 180°/n and central angle 360°/n. With side s the hexagon has area (3√3/2)s², circumradius s, and apothem (√3/2)s. A regular octagon of side 1 has area 2 + 2√2 ≈ 4.83. The diagonal of a regular pentagon is the golden ratio (1 + √5)/2 times its side (checked on a pentagon inscribed in the unit circle).

Try it

What is the interior angle of a regular 15-gon?

Answer. 13 · 180/15 = 156°.

Worked examples with Try-its: Using the circumscribed circle

A regular n-gon of circumradius R has area ½nR² sin(2π/n) and side 2R sin(π/n). Its inradius (apothem) is R cos(π/n). For a regular hexagon, six equilateral triangles of side s fit together, which is why R = s.

Try it

Find the area of a regular hexagon with side 2.

Answer. 6 · (√3/4) · 4 = 6√3.

Worked examples with Try-its: A longer problem

Problem. A regular hexagon of side 1 is inscribed in a circle. What is the area of the circle outside the hexagon? Solution. The circle has radius 1 and area π; the hexagon has area 3√3/2, so the difference is π − 3√3/2 ≈ 0.544.

Reflection. Decompose polygons into triangles from the centre.

More worked examples: Worked examples

A regular dodecagon inscribed in a unit circle has area ½ · 12 · sin 30° = 3. A regular 20-gon has interior angle 18 · 180°/20 = 162°. A regular hexagon inscribed in a unit circle has perimeter 6, and a regular octagon of side 1 has area 2 + 2√2.

Try it

How many diagonals has a regular decagon?

Answer. 35.

Try it

What is the exterior angle of a regular 18-gon?

Answer. 20°.

More worked examples: A second longer problem

Problem. The interior angle of a regular polygon is 150°. How many sides does it have? Solution. The exterior angle is 30°, and the exterior angles add to 360°, so n = 12.

Check. (12 − 2) · 180/12 = 150 ✓.

Practice problems (25)

Problem p1. How many diagonals does a regular 20-gon have?

Hintn(n − 3)/2.
Answer170
Method: Formula

20 · 17/2 = 170.

Method: Pairs

C(20,2) = 190 pairs of vertices minus 20 sides = 170.

Source: Andreescu & Gelca, 1.5

Problem p2. What is each interior angle, in degrees, of a regular 18-gon?

Hint(n − 2)·180/n.
Answer160
Method: Formula

16 · 180/18 = 160.

Method: Exterior

The exterior angle is 360/18 = 20, so the interior angle is 160.

Source: Andreescu & Gelca, 1.5

Problem p3. Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 156°. How many sides does it have?

HintExterior angle 24°.
Answer15
Method: Exterior angle

The exterior angle is 180 − 156 = 24°, so n = 360/24 = 15.

Method: Equation

(n − 2)·180/n = 156 gives 24n = 360.

Source: Andreescu & Gelca, 1.5

Problem p4. What is the area of a regular hexagon with side 4? (write like 24√3)

HintSix equilateral triangles.
Answer24√3
Method: Triangles

Each triangle of side 4 has area (√3/4)·16 = 4√3, so six give 24√3.

Method: Formula

(3√3/2)·16 = 24√3.

Source: Andreescu & Gelca, 1.5

Problem p5. A regular hexagon has circumradius 7. What is its side?

HintThe hexagon is six equilateral triangles with side equal to the radius.
Answer7
Method: Triangles

The triangle from the centre to two adjacent vertices has angle 60° and equal sides, so it is equilateral and the side is 7.

Method: Chord

The chord for a 60° arc is 2R sin 30° = R = 7.

Source: Andreescu & Gelca, 1.5

Problem p6. How many of the diagonals of a regular octagon pass through its centre?

HintJoining opposite vertices.
Answer4
Method: Opposite pairs

Each vertex has one opposite vertex, giving 8/2 = 4.

Method: Check

The diagonals through the centre are the 4 longest diagonals.

Source: Andreescu & Gelca, 1.5

Problem p7. Each exterior angle of a regular 40-gon is how many degrees?

360/40 = 9.

The interior angle is 171°, and 180 − 171 = 9.

Problem p8. A square is inscribed in a circle of radius 5. What is its area?

HintIts diagonal is the diameter.
Answer50

The diagonal is 10, so the area is d²/2 = 50.

Method: Side

The side is 5√2, and (5√2)² = 50.

Problem p9. How many equilateral triangles have their vertices among the vertices of a regular 12-gon?

HintTake every 4th vertex.
Answer4
Method: Starting points

The vertices k, k + 4, k + 8 form one for each k = 0, 1, 2, 3: 4 triangles.

Method: Reason

An equilateral triangle needs arcs of 120° = 4 steps between its vertices.

Source: Andreescu & Gelca, 1.5

Problem p12. A regular hexagon has side 4. How long is its longest diagonal?

HintIt passes through the centre.
Answer8

The distance from the centre to a vertex equals the side, so the diameter is 8.

The hexagon splits into 6 equilateral triangles of side 4.

Problem p17. How many isosceles triangles (including equilateral ones) can be formed using the vertices of a regular 12-gon?

HintChoose the apex, then a symmetric pair of other vertices.
Answer52
Method: Apex count

Each apex has 5 symmetric pairs, giving 12 · 5 = 60, but each of the 4 equilateral triangles was counted 3 times, so the total is 60 − 2 · 4 = 52.

Method: Check

The triples of vertices number 220, and 52 of them are isosceles.

Source: Andreescu & Gelca, 1.5
